How I work

  1. 01

    Embed with the customer

    I start in discovery with the people who own the workflow. Before anyone writes a prompt, we agree what "working" means: the data, the decisions and the success measures.

  2. 02

    Prototype in days, not quarters

    I build working agents and RAG prototypes with Vertex AI, Gemini, Claude, MCP and ADK, then iterate them live with stakeholders so we find problems early.

  3. 03

    Engineer for production

    Grounding, evaluation, identity and scoped permissions, observability and cost all get designed in from the start. My ten years in DevOps and security shape how I approach this.

  4. 04

    Turn wins into patterns

    I turn what worked into reference architectures, internal frameworks and delivery standards, so the next team ships faster and more consistently.
